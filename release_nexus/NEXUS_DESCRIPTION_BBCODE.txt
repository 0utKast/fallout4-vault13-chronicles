[size=4][b]☢️ PUBLIC BETA (v0.9.0) — WHAT IS COMPLETE TODAY & WHAT REMAINS ON THE ROADMAP[/b][/size]
[quote][b]Vault 13 Chronicles / Crónicas del Refugio 13[/b] is an ambitious, 100% standalone recreation of the original [b]1997 Fallout[/b] built directly on the Fallout 4 engine — packaged in two independent, fully voiced editions ([b]100% English[/b] and [b]100% Spanish[/b]) in the [b]Files[/b] tab.

• [b]100% Complete & Playable Right Now in v0.9.0 (Chapter 1 — [i]The Water Chip[/i]):[/b] From crossing the Vault-Tec Mnemosyne Temporal Portal in 2287 back to [b]December 5, 2161[/b], through [b]Vault 13[/b] (Overseer Jacoren & internal Vault subplots), [b]Shady Sands[/b], the buried ruins of [b]Vault 15[/b], [b]Garl's Khans & the 4-way Tandi Rescue[/b], [b]Junktown[/b] (Killian Darkwater, Captain Lars, Gizmo's taped confession, Neal & The Skulz, Doc Morbid), [b]The Hub[/b] (Water Merchants, Crimson Caravan Deathclaw & Tug, Sheriff Greene vs. Decker), frontier quests at [b]Lost Hills Exterior / The Glow[/b] & [b]L.A. Boneyard[/b], [b]Bakersfield / Necropolis Underground[/b], [b]The Watershed[/b] (Super Mutant Harry, the Great Water Pump moral choice & Vault 12 Water Chip extraction), all the way back to restoring Vault 13's Water Purification Plant — plus [b]all 3 classic companions[/b], [b]The Hub Radio (84.5 kHz)[/b], and [b]all 6 classic 1997 Special Encounters[/b].
• [b]In Development for v1.0 (Chapter 2 — [i]The Mutant Army & The Master[/i]):[/b] While exterior bunker landmarks and core records for Acts III–V are already built into the plugin, expanding and unlocking the full multi-level interiors of [b]Lost Hills Bunker[/b], [b]Adytum & Katja[/b], [b]Mariposa Military Base (FEV Vats)[/b], [b]The Cathedral[/b] (all 3 canonical Master victory paths), and the [b]May 10, 2162 Exile Epilogue[/b] is scheduled for [b]v1.0[/b]. Full progress is tracked openly on [url=https://github.com/0utKast/fallout4-vault13-chronicles]GitHub[/url].[/quote]

[*][b]Custom Open Worldspace — Southern California (2161) Fully Trackable on Your Pip-Boy:[/b]
  • A purpose-built [b]64-cell (8×8) open-world desert topography[/b] ([font=Courier New]V13SouthernCaliforniaWorld[/font]) stretching from the snow-dusted Sierra Madre peaks above [b]Vault 13[/b] and [b]Vault 15[/b] in the north, down through [b]Shady Sands[/b], [b]Junktown[/b], [b]Lost Hills[/b], and [b]The Hub[/b]'s central river valley, to the irradiated ruins of [b]Necropolis (Bakersfield)[/b], [b]The Glow (West Tek)[/b], [b]Mariposa[/b], and the [b]L.A. Boneyard / Cathedral[/b] in the south.
  • Includes a native 16-byte [font=Courier New].lod[/font] worldspace descriptor and a custom [b]2048×2048 12-mipmap classic Fallout 1 Pip-Boy World Map[/b] (localized in English and Spanish) so your [b]player cursor, active quest markers, custom location pins, and fast-travel targets align 1:1 on the Pip-Boy screen[/b] with zero F4SE or UI mods required!
[*][b]Full Chapter 1 Main Questline (Acts I & II — [i]The Water Chip[/i]):[/b]
  • Enter the [b]Vault-Tec Mnemosyne Historical Archive[/b] in the Commonwealth (northwest of Vault 111 / Sanctuary Hills) and step into [b]Vault 13 on December 5, 2161[/b], receiving [b]Overseer Jacoren[/b]'s iconic briefing: 150 days of drinking water remain.
  • Investigate [b]Shady Sands[/b] and descend into the collapsed subterranean command center of [b]Vault 15[/b].
  • Resolve internal Vault 13 crises (Calming [b]Theresa[/b]'s water-rationing faction, unmasking the [b]Water Thief[/b], and accessing Vault 13's armory).
  • Follow the water caravan trail to [b]Necropolis[/b]: parley with peaceful Vault 12 Ghouls in the Underground caverns, face [b]Set[/b] & [b]Garret[/b] in the Hall of the Dead, repair the [b]Great Water Pump[/b] so Necropolis doesn't die of thirst when you take the chip, confront Super Mutant [b]Harry, Barry & Sally[/b] at the [b]Watershed[/b] (bluff, fight, or sneak past), extract the [b]Water Chip[/b] in [b]Vault 12[/b], and install it in Vault 13's Purification Plant!
[*][b]True 1997 Multi-Path RPG Town & Wasteland Quests:[/b]
  • [b]Shady Sands & The Khans:[/b] Clear the [b]Emperor Radscorpion[/b] nest and cure poisoned farmer Jarvis with Razlo's antivenom; rescue [b]Tandi[/b] from Garl Death-Hand & Petrox's Khan camp via [b]4 classic RPG paths[/b] (combat assault, 500-cap ransom, speech intimidation, or lockpicking her cell door); liberate captives at Tolan's Eastern Pass; and recover the iconic [b].223 Pistol[/b] at [b]Irwin's Farm[/b].
  • [b]Junktown:[/b] Stop Gizmo's hired hitman [b]Kenji[/b] for Mayor [b]Killian Darkwater[/b] & Captain [b]Lars[/b]; wiretap or confront crime lord [b]Gizmo[/b] & his bodyguard [b]Izo[/b]; help bartender [b]Neal[/b] at the Skum Pitt recover his late wife's urn from [b]Vinnie[/b] (leader of [i]The Skulz[/i]); and uncover [b]Doc Morbid[/b]'s gruesome secret shipments to Iguana Bob!
  • [b]The Hub, Lost Hills Exterior & L.A. Boneyard:[/b] Escort [b]Master Merchant Martha Rastello[/b]'s water caravans; investigate the [b]Missing Caravans[/b] for Crimson Caravan, slay the [b]Deathclaw[/b], and interrogate dying Super Mutant [b]Tug[/b]; dismantle [b]Decker & Kane[/b]'s Underground syndicate at the Maltese Falcon with [b]Sheriff Justin Greene[/b]; recover the Ancient Brotherhood Holodisk from [b]ZAX-WT01 at The Glow[/b] for [b]Cabbot & Paladin Talus[/b] ([b]Wattz 2000 Laser Rifle[/b] reward); and clear the Boneyard Deathclaw brood for [b]Gabriel (Gun Runners)[/b], [b]Smitty[/b] ([b]Winchester P94 Plasma Caster[/b]), and [b]Nicole (Followers of the Apocalypse)[/b].
[*][b]3 Simultaneous Classic 2161 Companions (No Vanilla Companion Slot Replaced!):[/b]
  • Recruit [b]Ian[/b] in Shady Sands (100 Caps or Speech check), [b]Desert Ranger Tycho[/b] in Junktown, and [b]Dogmeat (2161)[/b] (using your Vault 13 jumpsuit or Iguana-on-a-Stick) — and [b]travel with all three at the same time[/b], complete with follow/wait/regroup commands and automatic Commonwealth suspension/restoration when crossing the Temporal Portal!
[*][b]Dedicated 2161 Radio Station — [i]The Hub Radio (84.5 kHz)[/i]:[/b]
  • Tune your Pip-Boy anywhere in 2161 Southern California to [b]The Hub Radio (84.5)[/b]: [b]24 original retro-atomic tracks[/b] paired with [b]24 voiced DJ news broadcasts[/b] reporting on Water Merchant caravans, Junktown's turf war, Radscorpion plagues, Brotherhood patrols, and rumors from Necropolis.
[*][b]All 6 Classic 1997 Special Encounters Hidden Across the Desert:[/b]
  1. [b]Unusual Call Box (1960s TARDIS):[/b] Dematerializes with its iconic wheezing groan & beacon flash when approached, leaving behind the functional [b]Pip-Boy Motion Sensor[/b]!
  2. [b]Crashed Flying Saucer ("Property of Area 51"):[/b] Recover the [b]1997 Classic Alien Blaster[/b] and the [b]Velvet Elvis Painting[/b].
  3. [b]Overturned Nuka-Cola Delivery Truck:[/b] Custom Nuka-Cola shipping crates and a hilarious pre-war Caps stash.
  4. [b]Giant Reptilian Footprint ("Godzilla"):[/b] Towering prehistoric crater concealing a [b]Stealth Boy[/b] on a crushed skeleton.
  5. [b]Bob's Pre-Owned Car Mart:[/b] Abandoned dealership housing the legendary high-crit [b]Red Ryder LE BB Gun[/b].
  6. [b]Patrick the Celt:[/b] Friendly wandering bard whose song of Old Erin grants [b]+1 Permanent Charisma[/b]!

You can travel freely between 2161 California and 2287 Commonwealth at any time.
